Welcome to My Poetry

I'm DJ Usman, a poet sharing my thoughts and emotions through words. This space is dedicated to my Urdu poetry and creative expressions. Every poem reflects emotions, memories, love, pain, hope, and the beautiful journey of life.

My Poetry Collection

Kitni Raatein Beet Gayin

Kitni raatein beet gayin, kitne din dhal gaye.
Jinhein kabhi badalna na tha, wahi log badal gaye.

Time passes, nights fade into days,
Those who were never meant to change, changed anyway.

Jo Mukammal Milte Nahi

Jo mukammal milte nahi,
Woh mukammal bichhadte bhi nahi.

Some souls never truly meet,
Yet they never completely part.

Khamoshi

Khamoshi bhi ajeeb zubaan hoti hai,
Har baat alfaazon ki mohtaaj nahi hoti.

Silence has its own language,
Not every feeling needs words.

Yaadein

Kuch yaadein muskura deti hain,
Aur kuch aankhein nam kar deti hain.

Some memories make us smile,
Others silently bring tears.

Mohabbat

Mohabbat lafzon ki mohtaaj nahi hoti,
Dil se nikle toh dua ban jaati hai.

Love doesn't depend on words,
When it comes from the heart, it becomes a prayer.

Waqt

Waqt sab kuch sikha deta hai,
Bas logon ki pehchaan der se hoti hai.

Time teaches every lesson,
It only takes time to know people.

Musafir

Hum bhi musafir the is zindagi ke,
Kuch log mile, kuch yaadein reh gayin.

We were travelers in this journey of life,
Some people stayed, some became memories.

Dil

Dil tootne ki awaaz nahi hoti,
Magar uska dard umr bhar rehta hai.

A broken heart makes no sound,
Yet its pain lasts a lifetime.

Intezaar

Kuch intezaar kabhi khatam nahi hote,
Bas insaan jeena seekh leta hai.

Some waits never truly end,
We simply learn how to live with them.

Zindagi

Zindagi har roz naya sabak deti hai,
Jo samajh le wahi kamyaab hota hai.

Life teaches a new lesson every day,
Success belongs to those who learn.
