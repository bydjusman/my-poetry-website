## WHAT THE FORM DOES

Users can:
1. Enter name, email, subject, message
2. Click "Send Message"
3. Form validates all fields
4. Shows errors if invalid
5. Submits to Formspree if valid
6. Formspree forwards to your email
7. User sees success message
8. Form clears for next submission
